# Keeping LoftRest safe and legal

A checklist for LoftRest™ as an Australian online store. It covers the main rules that apply to a product like this. It is not legal advice. If you're unsure about something, ask a lawyer or the regulator named below.

## 1. Health claims (TGA)

In Australia, a product advertised to relieve pain or treat a condition counts as a **medical device**. It must be registered on the ARTG before it's sold, and its ads must follow the TGA Advertising Code. LoftRest is sold as a **comfort and relaxation product**, so its wording must stay that way.

| OK to say | Don't say |
| --- | --- |
| relax, unwind, soothing, comfortable, gentle stretch | relieves / reduces neck pain, pain relief |
| all-night neck support, contoured memory foam | decompresses the spine, spinal decompression, pinched nerves |
| relaxation session, modes, warmth, massage | therapy, therapeutic, clinical, treatment, heals, cures |
| made for desk workers and physical workers | fixes / corrects posture, reverses tech neck |
| helps you get comfortable at night | treats insomnia, stops headaches |
| | recommended or used by chiropractors, physios or doctors |

This applies everywhere: your website, product description, ads, social posts, emails, and captions on images. Pictures and videos count as claims too.

If you ever want to make pain-relief claims, you'll need to include LoftRest on the ARTG first, through a sponsor, and follow the TGA Advertising Code. See tga.gov.au.

## 2. Prices and sales (ACCC)

- Only show a crossed-out "was" price if you **actually sold at that price** for a reasonable period beforehand.
- No fake countdown timers, "only 3 left" messages or "stock strictly limited" unless they're true.
- Discounts must work exactly as advertised. The LOFTREST2PACK code gives 10% off 2 or more.
- Shipping promises must match your Shopify shipping settings. Right now that's free standard shipping in Australia and A$20 international.

## 3. Reviews and endorsements (ACCC)

- Only publish **genuine** reviews from real customers. Never write your own, pay for positive ones, or hide negative ones.
- No made-up testimonials, "as seen in" logos or social proof you can't back up ("thousands of customers").
- Content must be clearly your own brand's. Don't dress up ads as independent news articles.

## 4. Customer rights (Australian Consumer Law)

- Faulty, unsafe or not-as-described items must get a repair, replacement or refund. You can't opt out of this.
- Keep your refund, shipping and terms of service policies in line with what you actually do (the 30-day change-of-mind returns included).

## 5. Business registration

- **ABN:** apply at abr.gov.au if you haven't already, then add it to your legal notice and terms.
- **Business name:** if you trade as "LoftRest" and that isn't your own personal or company name, register the business name with ASIC.
- **GST:** register once your turnover reaches A$75,000 a year.
- **Trademark (optional):** "™" is fine to use. Registering LoftRest with IP Australia lets you use ® and protects the name.

## 6. Product safety

- Ask your supplier for safety test reports for the product, and for any charger or cable you supply.
- A USB **wall adapter** sold in Australia must meet electrical safety rules and carry the **RCM** mark. If you don't include an adapter, say "adapter not included".
- Include clear instructions and warnings in the box.
- If you learn of a safety problem, you must report it to the ACCC within 2 days (productsafety.gov.au) and consider a recall.

## 7. Images and content

- Use only photos and videos you own, generated yourself, or have a licence for. Don't use images saved from Pinterest or other stores.
- Keep a record of where each image came from.

## 8. Privacy and marketing

- Your privacy policy is in place (Shopify). Keep it up to date.
- Only send marketing emails or SMS to people who have **opted in**, and always include an unsubscribe option (Spam Act).

## Before you publish anything new

1. Does it make a health or pain claim? Rewrite it as comfort and relaxation.
2. Is every number true (price, discount, shipping, delivery time)?
3. Are reviews, photos and quotes genuine and yours to use?
4. Does it match your policies?
