This policy covers everything you buy from LoftRest™, including the LoftRest™ pillow and sleep mask.

1. Change-of-mind returns (30 days)

If you change your mind, you can return an item within 30 days of delivery for a refund, as long as:
- it is unused, with no signs of use, wear, marks or odours
- it is in its original packaging, with all parts, cables and accessories
- you include your order number or proof of purchase

For change-of-mind returns:
- you pay the return shipping, and we recommend a tracked service, because the item is your responsibility until it reaches us
- original shipping charges are not refunded
- items that have been used, or that don't meet the conditions above, can't be refunded for change of mind; we'll send them back to you at your cost

2. Faulty, damaged or incorrect items

Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. You are entitled to a replacement or refund for a major failure and compensation for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. You are also entitled to have the goods repaired or replaced if the goods fail to be of acceptable quality and the failure does not amount to a major failure.

If your item arrives faulty, damaged or not as described, or develops a fault, contact us. We will pay the return shipping and arrange a repair, replacement or refund. These rights apply whether or not the item has been used, whether or not it was bought on sale, and they are not limited to 30 days.

3. How to start a return

1. Email koscigear@outlook.com with your order number and the reason for the return. For a faulty or damaged item, please include a photo or short video.
2. We'll reply within 2 business days with the return address and instructions.
3. Please don't send anything back before contacting us, so we can match your return to your order.

4. Refunds

When your return arrives, we'll inspect it and email you to confirm whether the refund is approved. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 10 business days. Your bank or card provider may take a few extra days to show it.

5. Exchanges

To swap an item, return it under this policy and place a new order.
