30-day change-of-mind returns
If you change your mind, you can return LoftRest™ within 30 days of delivery for a refund. To be eligible, the item must be in its original condition and packaging, unused beyond trying it out, and returned with proof of purchase.

For change-of-mind returns, you are responsible for the cost of return shipping. Original shipping charges are not refundable.

Faulty, damaged or incorrect items
Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. You are entitled to a replacement or refund for a major failure and compensation for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. You are also entitled to have the goods repaired or replaced if the goods fail to be of acceptable quality and the failure does not amount to a major failure.

If your LoftRest™ arrives faulty, damaged or not as described, contact us within a reasonable time and we will cover return shipping and arrange a repair, replacement or refund.

How to start a return
Email us at koscigear@outlook.com with your order number and the reason for the return. We will reply with return instructions. Please do not send items back without contacting us first.

Refunds
Once we receive and inspect your return, we will email you to confirm whether it has been approved. Approved refunds are made to your original payment method, usually within 10 business days. Your bank or card provider may take a few extra days to process it.
