LoftRest™ is a 3-in-1 cervical traction pillow for your evening wind-down. Lie back for a relaxing 15–30 minute session of gentle stretch, warmth and massage, then rest or drift off on its contoured shape.

Pneumatic air traction: built-in air chambers gently lift and lengthen the neck for a soothing stretch you control.

Constant-temperature heat: steady, soothing warmth at the base of your neck.

Micro-kinetic massage: gentle massage modes to help you unwind. Use them with the stretch and warmth, or on their own.

Contoured comfort: a contoured shape with a soft, breathable flannel cover, made to cradle your neck.

Specifications

Size: 42.5 × 27 cm (16.7 × 10.6 in)
Cover: soft, breathable flannel
Power: 5V USB (works with a USB wall adapter, laptop or power bank)
Safety: built-in automatic shut-off timer

Talk to your doctor before use if you are pregnant, have had neck or spine surgery, or have osteoporosis, a recent neck injury, a known spinal condition or a circulation disorder. LoftRest™ is a comfort and relaxation product, not a medical device, and is not designed to diagnose, treat or relieve any medical condition.
