Most at-home neck products do one thing: a foam stretcher or a heat pack. LoftRest™ runs three together, or one at a time:

It’s an easy way to unwind your neck at home, whenever you need it, with no appointments.

2

It targets “tech neck” and physical strain

Looking down at a screen, phone or workbench pushes your head forward. An often-cited estimate is that every inch forward adds around 10 pounds (4.5 kg) of load on your neck. Over a long day, that leaves the muscles tight and sore.
